I create video and digital media with a focus on storytelling, pacing, and audience retention. My work ranges from theatre audio-visual production and gallery media to editing short-form and long-form content for online creators. I'm interested in how visual media moves between spaces, from live performance environments to social platforms and digital storytelling. I graduated from RMIT University with a degree in Digital Media, where I developed both the creative and technical skills that shape my work today.
